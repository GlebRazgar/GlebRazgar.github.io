Information is the fundamental currency of the world.
It is the unit of exchange underlying money, relationships and energy.
Dollars, in all their capitalistic might, abstract away much richer information about whatever is being sold or purchased. "A bottle of wine aged for 10 years in Venice" can be compressed down to a $100 figure. A single number that encodes something about the effort of the seller and the demand of the consumer.
In relationships, years of shared experiences can be averaged into a single emotion like love.
And even in physics, information is often considered more fundamental than energy or matter; for  no system can be described without it.
This is all to say that underneath all the abstractions we deal with on daily basis, there is often many more layers to the informational onion, and the more you peel it, the closer you get to the ground truth.

To that end, i romanticise about becoming closer to the truth. Becoming an information trillionaire!

**II. What is information richness?**  
Since I yield the all-mighty quill, I will define informational wealth here as the possession of **useful** information. Or more rigorously: information that allows you to better predict the future.

By that standard, a tic-tock kid sponging up bits -- scroll by scroll --  isn't becoming the wealthiest mind in town, regardless of how many bits pass before his eyes, because little of that scrolling is likely retained at the end of the day. A bit like pouring money into a pocket with a pocket-sized hole in it.
But, on the opposite extreme, a world memory champion might still be in the informational lower-class, since facts remembering many facts can still be useless, or in other words have low predictive power. As you might know from school, memorising textbooks often doesn't help on tests.
Notice that these two fail at different points. The scroller takes in enormous volume and retains almost none of it. The memoriser retains everything but can't turn it into utility.

Well... If these two are not prime examples of informationally minted archetypes, what would be?

Stripping it to first principles, humans interact with information in three ways: via acquisition, processing and transmission.
People that efficiently aquire information, turn it into useful models, and leverage these models to aquire the subsequent information cheaper than the prior, tend to be very good at predicting the future and in my eyes are informationally rich.

The TikTok addict has enormous informational income but terrible savings. The memory champion has an impressive vault, but the assets inside it may produce little return. The information trillionaire, by contrast, owns a dense network of models whose value compounds as each new piece of information finds its place among the old.

Once you understand probability, a hundred observations about uncertainty collapse into a handful of principles. Once you understand evolution, thousands of biological facts stop being isolated facts. Once you understand incentives, seemingly unrelated behaviours in markets, politics, companies, and relationships begin to rhym.

That is the kind of wealth I romanticise accumulating.
