In capitalism, money is the fundamental unit of exchange. A simple metric to go by.
But information is the currency of the world.

From dollars, to the relationships, information describes the world.
And with the ability to store and modify information, you gain the ability to model and shape the world. A neat skill to-say-the-least!

To that end, i romanticise becoming an information trillionaire.
But what would that even look like?
Unlike with money, information richness is tricky to define. For all you know, some tic-tock kid sponging up bits -- scroll by scroll --  is the wealthiest mind in town.
Perhaps.
But since i'm wielding the quill, informational-wealth here will roughly ≈ intelligence = the ability to predict the future. The richer you are in information and it's processing capacity, the better your predictive power.

Remaining question:
1. How do I measure it.
2. How I can become one -- the infinity stones of info-richness
